# AI disclosure

This plugin is **human-led and AI-assisted**. The maintainer (**game25011**) builds it together
with a **DeepSeek Harness** coding agent.

What that means in practice:

- The **human maintainer** sets the requirements and scope, reviews every change, performs the
  real-device testing (PC, the Tailscale link, and the phone/tablet browser), decides on releases
  and publishes them.
- The **AI agent** writes most of the code, the tests and the documentation, under that review.
- Screenshots in the README were captured on the maintainer's own devices. The tailnet host name,
  the login token, the QR content and the local workspace name are masked or not shown.

The unit tests, the source and [docs/TEST-REPORT.md](docs/TEST-REPORT.md) are the evidence.
AI-generated content can be wrong; do not rely on the README alone when evaluating
security-sensitive behavior.
